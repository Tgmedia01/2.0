Cookie policy

This site does not set cookies of its own, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking running on it. There is nothing here to consent to, which is why you have not been asked.

What a cookie is

A small file a website asks your browser to keep, so it can recognise the browser later. Most are harmless; the ones people object to are those that follow you between sites to build an advertising profile.

What this site uses

Nothing of that kind. No cookie is written by this site. Two things still involve a third party, and neither tracks you across the web:

Fonts
Typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google. No cookie is set by that request.
Enquiry form
Sending the form posts your message to Formspree, which handles spam filtering and forwards it by email. Details are in the privacy policy.
Server logs
The hosting provider records ordinary request logs. That is infrastructure, not a cookie, and it is not used to profile visitors.

If that ever changes

If analytics or any other non-essential cookie is added later, you will be asked before it loads, not after, and this page will say what it is and what it does. Nothing non-essential runs on the basis of an assumed yes.

Managing cookies yourself

Every browser lets you see and delete stored cookies and block them per site: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge. Blocking everything will not break this site.
