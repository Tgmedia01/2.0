Stone worktops, sold from a showroom and online. The material is the product, so the website has to show it honestly and then get out of the way of the enquiry.

Sector
Stone worktops
Type
Online retail
Scope
Design, build
Site
purplegranite.co.uk

What the site has to do

Nobody buys a worktop from a thumbnail.

Stone is chosen on colour, pattern and edge, and photographed badly it all looks the same. The site’s job is to let someone compare materials properly, understand what a slab will look like in a kitchen, and then either buy or ask a question without hunting for the form. Product photography does the persuading; the interface stays quiet enough to let it.

Material first

Large, honest imagery of the stone itself, at a size where pattern and edge detail actually read.

Comparison without a maze

Materials sit side by side, so choosing feels like choosing rather than searching.

One step to ask

An enquiry is never more than a move away, wherever someone happens to be looking.

Full notes for this project are being written up with the client before they are published here. The live site is the best look at it in the meantime.

Material and product photography to be supplied by the client
