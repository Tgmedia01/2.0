What the site has to do

A publisher’s website is read by three different people who all arrive at the same front door.

A writer wants to know who they would be working with. A label or supervisor wants the catalogue and a licensing route. Everyone else wants to understand what the company actually is inside two sentences.

Publishing is a catalogue business, so the structure matters more than the surface: what is held, who handles it, and how to ask about using it. Get that hierarchy right and the design has something solid to sit on.

Full notes for this project, covering the brief, the design decisions and the technical approach, are being written up with the client before they are published here. The live site is the best look at it in the meantime.
