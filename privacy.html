Privacy policy

Short version: the only personal information this site collects is what you type into the enquiry form, and it is used to reply to you. There is no tracking, no advertising and no mailing list.

Who is responsible

TG Media is an independent design studio run by Tom McTigue, based in Warwick, United Kingdom. TG Media is the data controller for information submitted through this site. For anything about your data, email tom@tgmedia.uk.

What is collected, and why

If you send an enquiry, the form collects your name, email address and whatever you write in it — along with the optional details you choose to give, such as your organisation, current website, project type, timing and budget range.

That information is used for one purpose: to read your enquiry and reply to it, and to carry on the conversation if there is one to have. The lawful basis is legitimate interests — responding to someone who has deliberately contacted a business — supported by the consent you give when you tick the box on the form.

Your details are not sold, shared for marketing, or added to any list. You will not receive a newsletter because there is not one.

Third parties involved

Formspree
Processes enquiry-form submissions and forwards them by email. See Formspree’s privacy policy.
Google Fonts
Serves the typefaces used on this site, which means Google receives a request from your browser including your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.
Hosting
The site is served by a third-party hosting provider, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and diagnostics.
Email
Enquiries arrive in, and are kept in, an ordinary business email account.

There is no analytics product running on this site, no advertising pixels and no third-party embeds beyond the above.

How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept while there is a live conversation and for a reasonable period afterwards, in case the project comes back around. Where an enquiry becomes a project, correspondence is kept for as long as needed for that work and for normal business and tax records. Ask for your enquiry to be deleted and it will be.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to it being used, or ask for its use to be restricted. Email tom@tgmedia.uk and it will be dealt with directly, normally within a few days and always within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Related: the cookie policy and the terms of use.
