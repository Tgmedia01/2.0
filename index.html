Slab, not swatch. Edge profile. Compared side by side. Then asked about, in one step.

The studio

TG Media is one person, and that is the point.

You talk to me and I do the work — strategy, design, the front end, the CMS. Nothing is relayed through an account manager and nothing is lost between a design file and a developer, because the person drawing it is the person building it.

No house style pushed onto every client. A music publisher and a stone specialist should not end up with the same website in different colours.

Photographers, writers and specialists come in when they genuinely make the project better. I stay responsible for the result either way.
