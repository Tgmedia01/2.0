Work  /  01  /  Case study

Lead with
the work.

Bar Studios is a production studio built around social content. Their output is documentary, short form, campaign work and live event coverage, judged on quality by brand and agency clients who look at a portfolio for about thirty seconds. The site had one job: put the work in front of those people at the scale it deserves.

The situation

A production studio is only as convincing as the last thing a client watched.

The work was strong and the client list was strong. The website sat behind both, so the studio was being assessed on the weakest thing it owned. Everything that follows came out of fixing that one imbalance rather than from a general redesign.

The response  /  three decisions

The work leads

Every structural decision serves getting someone to the best content quickly, in an environment that lets it play at scale. The studio introduces itself second.

An archive that varies

Project placements are deliberately unequal in size. Flagship campaigns take the larger frames, so the grid reads as curation rather than a feed.

One step to contact

New business is never more than one move away from wherever a visitor happens to be watching.

Visual system

A design system that stays out of the way of the film.

Monochrome throughout, with colour arriving only through the client’s own footage. The typography is extended and confidently weighted; the layout borrows from editorial publishing rather than digital convention.

Colour
Near-black and off-white only. Everything chromatic comes from the footage.
Typography
Extended sans, wide tracking, high contrast between headline and caption.
Motion
Featured work autoplays muted and looped. Hover reveals title and category, nothing more.

Mobile and build

Most of this audience is watching on a phone, on a train, on a poor connection.

So the mobile experience is designed rather than inherited: video is cropped for a vertical frame, poster images stand in until playback is ready, and loading adapts to the connection instead of assuming office broadband. The archive was built on a custom CMS so the team can add work, edit case studies and reorder the homepage without needing me.
