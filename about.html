How I work

I like a straightforward brief and a straight conversation. Early on I will ask more questions than you expect. Once we agree what the site is for, I would rather show you real pages than mood boards.

Technical decisions get made on what the project actually needs. Sometimes that is a fast custom build; often it is WordPress because your team needs to publish on a Friday afternoon without me. I will tell you which and why, in language that does not need a developer to translate.

When something genuinely needs another pair of hands, such as photography, film or a specialist integration, I bring in people I have worked with before and stay responsible for the result. What I do not do is quietly hand your project to someone else.

You do not need to know what the site should look like. That is my job. You should know what the business is trying to do.

If what you need is the cheapest possible template by Friday, I am the wrong person, and I will say so rather than take the work.

Who the work suits

Businesses with real character and a website that no longer shows it.

Publishers, production studios and creative companies, and equally a salon, a stone specialist or a company that simply makes things properly. The common factor is not the sector. It is that the business has substance and the presentation has fallen behind it.
